Three design directions for the BaseRouter home screen

A · Socket

BaseRouter is a power strip. Providers plug in at the top, every route is an outlet with its own switch. When a budget runs out, the breaker trips.

Open full screen

B · Display

BaseRouter is a small desk device. One screen that tells you everything, a chunky key per route, and a pixel creature living on the display.

Open full screen

C · Home

BaseRouter is home base. A homing pigeon carries the requests, the one address is the plate on the door, and every route is a room with its lights on or off.
